PROMPT_2: Explain quantization entanglement in simple terms
REFERENCE: Quantization entanglement refers to the interdependency between quantization errors across different layers of a neural network. When one layer is quantized, the resulting rounding errors propagate through subsequent layers, creating a compounding effect that can degrade model accuracy. Managing this entanglement is key to achieving high-quality quantized models.
